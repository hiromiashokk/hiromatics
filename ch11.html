When do two numbers share common ground? Let's meet HCF and LCM — the ideas behind tiles, gift bags and blinking lights.

Growing strong — I'm Sprout.

Let's dig up the factors and multiples that numbers share.

A factor divides a number evenly. A multiple is what you get in its times table.

Factors of 12 are 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12 — the group sizes that share 12 sweets with none left over. Multiples of 4 are 4, 8, 12, 16…

Joke: Why was 6 so popular? It had loads of factors — everyone was a big fan-tor! 😄

The HCF (Highest Common Factor) is the biggest number that divides both.

For a 12 ft × 16 ft room, the largest square tile that fits with no cutting is 4 ft — the HCF of 12 and 16. Same idea as packing the biggest identical gift bags from two sweet piles.

The LCM (Lowest Common Multiple) is the smallest number both divide into.

Two lights blink every 4 s and every 6 s. They flash together every 12 s — the LCM of 4 and 6. Handy for buses, alarms and traffic lights!

A quick trick: break each number into its prime factors, then pick shared primes for HCF and all primes for LCM.

Fun fact: For any two numbers, HCF × LCM = the two numbers multiplied together. Find one and the other pops out! 🧮
Joke: Why did the two numbers go to therapy? To work out their common factors. 🌱
