Learn

First, see how two fractions multiply as a picture. Shade one fraction across the rows and the other down the columns. The overlap is the product.

Hi! I'm Pochi.

I'll explain how the shaded overlap becomes the fraction product.

Split the rectangle into equal rows for the first fraction and columns for the second.

For example, 3/4 means 3 shaded rows out of 4, and 2/5 means 2 shaded columns out of 5.

The cells shaded by both the rows and the columns are double-shaded.

These double-shaded cells are the product of the two fractions.

In this picture, 3 rows × 2 columns gives 6 double-shaded cells.

The whole rectangle has 4 × 5 = 20 cells, so the product is 6/20.

Now simplify the overlap fraction: 6/20 = 3/10.

In Build It, you'll match the target product by shading rows and columns the right way.
