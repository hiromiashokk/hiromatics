Learn

Numbers tell stories once you connect the dots! Let's learn mean, median and mode — and how charts turn data into pictures.

Ahoy — I'm Pip.

Let's dig up the treasure hiding in a pile of numbers.

The mean (average) is: add everything up, then divide by how many there are.

A batter scores 2, 4 and 6 runs in three matches. Mean = (2 + 4 + 6) ÷ 3 = 4 runs per match.

Joke: Why did the average feel stretched? It got pulled around by every single value! 😄

The median is the middle value once you sort the numbers from small to big.

Line the class up by height and the person right in the middle marks the median. For 1, 3, 9 the median is 3.

The mode is the value that shows up most often.

If most kids in class wear size 5 shoes, then 5 is the mode. A shop stocks the mode because it sells the most!

Charts turn numbers into pictures: a bar chart compares amounts, a pie chart shows parts of a whole.

Fun fact: One giant value can drag the mean way up, but the median barely budges — that's why news reports often use the median! 📊
Joke: Why was the mode so popular? It just kept showing up! 🥒
