Let's learn it first

A quick story, then a couple of checks — no zooming required yet.

Sonu's mum was fixing a toy with two screws that looked identical. She grabbed one... and it didn't fit!

🔩 🔩

To tell them apart, whole numbers aren't precise enough. We need a smaller unit.

Zoom in on the gap between 2 and 3. It splits into 10 equal tenths.

Landing on the 7th little step gives 2.7 — two whole units and seven tenths.

Zoom in again, on that one tenth between 2.7 and 2.8. It splits into 10 more equal parts — hundredths.

Landing on the 5th of those gives 2.75 — two units, seven tenths, five hundredths.

That's the whole idea of decimals: every zoom splits a part into 10 smaller parts — units → tenths → hundredths → thousandths, forever.
