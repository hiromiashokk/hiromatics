Let's hunt for matching triangle twins and learn how to spot a fake.

Congruent shapes have the same shape AND the same size. Slide one on top of the other — even if you flip or turn it — and it fits exactly.

Think identical twins, or two cookies from the same cookie cutter. Same in every way!

Joke: What did one congruent triangle say to the other? “You're just my type... exactly!” 😄

A triangle has 6 measurements (3 sides + 3 angles), but you don't need all of them. These shortcuts lock it in:

• SSS — all three sides match
• SAS — two sides and the angle between them
• ASA — two angles and the side between them

Match any one of these and the twins are guaranteed!

Here's the trap: two sides and an angle that is not between them can build two different triangles.

That's exactly why Rabia got two shapes from the same measurements — the clues weren't enough to pin just one down.

Congruence is how factories make thousands of identical parts, and how patterns repeat perfectly.

Fun fact: Rangoli and floor-tile designs rely on congruent shapes fitting together — flip, turn, repeat, and it still matches! 🪔
