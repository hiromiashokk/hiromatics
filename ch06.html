Numbers love to play! Let's explore magic squares and the odd-even rules that make number puzzles tick.

Let's play some mischievous number games and find the patterns hiding inside.

A magic square uses each number 1–9 once so that every row, every column, and both diagonals add up to the SAME total — the magic sum.

It's like Sudoku's cousin: instead of "no repeats", the rule is "same sum everywhere".

Joke: Why was 6 afraid of 7? Because 7 “8” 9! 😄

Why is the magic sum 15? Add 1+2+3+…+9 = 45. There are 3 rows, and they share the total equally: 45 ÷ 3 = 15.

A neat twist: the number 5 always sits in the centre, because it's the middle value that balances every line.

Parity means odd or even. An even number splits into two equal groups (share sweets between 2 friends, none left over). An odd number always leaves one out.

The rules: odd + odd = even, even + even = even, odd + even = odd. Two odds pair up perfectly!

Once you spot parity, you can predict answers without even adding — that's the power of number patterns.

Fun fact: The 3×3 magic square (called Lo Shu) is over 4000 years old. Legend says it appeared on the back of a turtle in a Chinese river! 🐢
Joke: Why did 2 feel lonely at the odd numbers' party? It was the only even one there. 🥲
