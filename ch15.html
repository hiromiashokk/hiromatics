Weigh the unknown

The chapter opens with a weighing scale: two pans, some numbers, one rule — if the scale is level, both sides weigh the same. Tap a known weight to lift it off both pans at once. The scale stays level the whole time — that is the entire trick to finding x.

x + 3 = 7
Tap a known-weight chip on the left pan to lift it off both sides.

Solve It

Now do it without the pans — the same idea works on paper. Whatever you do to one side of an equation, do to the other, until only the letter is left standing alone.
